# Accessibility release checklist

- Primary text is at least 18sp-equivalent and remains usable at 200% scaling.
- Every touch target is at least 48×48dp; primary actions target 56dp.
- Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast and do not rely on color alone.
- TalkBack and VoiceOver announce screen titles, controls, exercise instructions, progress, and feedback.
- Focus order follows visual order and returns predictably after dialogs.
- Animations respect reduced-motion preference; nothing flashes.
- Timed exercises weight accuracy over speed and never show a stressful countdown.
- All audio information is also visible; narration and sounds can be controlled separately.
- The smallest supported phone works with the largest supported text setting.
- iOS Dynamic Type and Android font scaling remain usable at 200%.
- Quick Sums exposes its typed-number alternative to VoiceOver and never requires handwriting as the only input method.
